GIROTTI

International performance marketing and e-commerce advertising for a customizable footwear brand.

Project Overview

GIROTTI is an international fashion e-commerce brand specializing in customizable footwear and accessories.

Industry
Fashion e-commerce
Focus
International performance marketing and e-commerce advertising

My Role

I managed international advertising, optimized campaigns and product selections, analyzed performance, and coordinated with technical collaborators on product data and measurement.

Challenges

A customizable catalog spans categories, languages, and markets. Campaign analysis needed to connect market-level results with the product data and promotions behind them, while accounting for differences in tracking and reporting.

Strategy and Approach

I compared campaign and product-level data to group products, tailor advertising by market, and prioritize feed and copy changes. I worked with technical collaborators to investigate tracking and attribution questions.

Key Areas of Work

  • International Google Ads campaign management across Search, Shopping, and Performance Max.
  • Market-specific optimization, catalog segmentation, and product data improvements in Merchant Center and supplemental feeds.
  • Promotional advertising, multilingual ad content, and campaign performance analysis.
  • Marketing reporting initiatives using BigQuery, SQL, and Looker Studio.

My Contribution

Completed work. I grouped products for advertising and promotions, reviewed product titles and descriptions, and supported localized ad copy and reporting. I investigated tracking and attribution questions with technical collaborators.

Research and recommendations. I evaluated product video and enhanced feed capabilities and developed recommendations for follow-up.

Qualitative Outcomes

Product selections were organized by advertising need. Reporting made campaign and product data easier to review together, and tracking questions were identified for follow-up.

Tools and technologies

  • Google Ads
  • Google Merchant Center
  • Supplemental feeds
  • BigQuery
  • SQL
  • Looker Studio
