About me

I’m Lora Cholakova, a performance marketing specialist based in Sofia, Bulgaria. My work brings together international e-commerce, agency leadership, and hands-on measurement.

My background

My background spans paid media, agency leadership, and international e-commerce. At Advertise BG and Digitas Sofia, I led a performance team and worked with media, creative, development, and analytics colleagues. At Heat Digital, I focus on international Google Ads campaigns, product feeds, reporting, and technical diagnostics.

I bring that leadership experience to hands-on work. I start by asking what decision the team needs to make, then check whether campaign, product, and conversion data can answer it. When a feed or tracking issue affects what we can see, I work with the people who own that part of the system to investigate it.

I study computing and computer science at Varna Free University. That work complements what I do in marketing, especially when a question involves querying data, understanding how platforms exchange information, or checking whether a report reflects the events it claims to measure.

What I look at first

Market context

What changes when the same catalog is advertised in another language or market? Search behavior, local copy, and product data can each affect which campaigns make sense.

Measurement

What does a conversion represent, and can we compare it across systems? I start with event definitions and data quality before drawing conclusions from a report.

Follow-through

What can be addressed in the campaign, and what needs a feed, analytics, or development change? I make these handoffs as specific as I can.
