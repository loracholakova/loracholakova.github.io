AEROSUS

International performance marketing and product advertising for an automotive aftermarket specialist.

Project Overview

AEROSUS specializes in air suspension products and replacement parts for the automotive aftermarket.

Industry
Automotive e-commerce
Focus
International performance marketing and product advertising

My Role

I managed and optimized international campaigns, supported product advertising and market launch preparation, and investigated feed and tracking issues with technical and marketing teams.

Challenges

Buyers may search by technical terms or original equipment manufacturer (OEM) part numbers. Across markets and languages, product attributes and part identifiers needed to be consistent enough for relevant ads and reliable diagnostics.

Strategy and Approach

I researched keywords and local search intent, then used that research to structure campaigns and localized copy. I reviewed queries for negative keyword opportunities and checked product data, delivery, and conversion signals with technical teams and advertising partners.

Key Areas of Work

  • International Google Ads management across Search, Shopping, and Performance Max.
  • Market launch preparation, OEM-focused Search, keyword and negative keyword optimization, and localized content.
  • Merchant Center troubleshooting, product feed diagnostics, and tracking and analytics checks.
  • Affiliate performance analysis and collaboration with technical and marketing teams.

My Contribution

I prepared campaign activity for new markets, developed OEM-focused Search campaigns, and reviewed search terms and negative keywords. I troubleshot product feeds and Merchant Center issues, checked conversion tracking and analytics, and analyzed affiliate performance.

Qualitative Outcomes

Product data discrepancies were resolved, market-specific activity was prepared, and opportunities to refine targeting and measurement were identified.

Tools and technologies

  • Google Ads
  • Google Merchant Center
  • Product feeds
  • Search term reports
  • Conversion tracking
