Areas of expertise

Performance marketing

Marketing technology & tracking

Conversion tracking checks, tagging and attribution diagnostics, and collaboration with developers and analytics teams to investigate measurement gaps.

From campaign review to action

I check the campaign objective and the quality of the data first. A delivery issue can start in the campaign settings, product data, or eligibility rules. A reporting issue calls for a closer look at what was measured and how.

Once I identify a likely cause, I document what I found, what can be changed in the advertising platform, and what needs input from a developer or another team.
